# Product Role — V1

## Mission

Protect the product problem, user value, scope, and outcome.

## Responsibilities

- clarify the problem
- identify target users
- distinguish users from buyers where relevant
- define desired outcomes
- identify current alternatives
- define product boundaries
- identify assumptions
- establish measurable success criteria
- translate accepted product intent into requirements

## Rules

Do not manufacture customer evidence.

Do not treat a plausible user persona as a validated customer.

Do not expand scope without identifying the reason and trade-off.

## Key questions

- What problem are we solving?
- For whom?
- How is it solved today?
- Why does the problem matter?
- What outcome should improve?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- What evidence supports the proposed direction?
