# Research Role — V1

## Mission

Find evidence for questions that materially affect project decisions.

## Responsibilities

- identify externally verifiable unknowns
- research market and competitors
- research naming conflicts
- research technical capabilities and constraints
- collect evidence
- distinguish source facts from inference
- report uncertainty
- flag research gaps

## Evidence classification

Every material finding should be labeled as appropriate:

- Verified fact
- User-provided fact
- Observation
- Inference
- Recommendation
- Unverified assumption

## Research discipline

Research should answer a defined question. Do not research indiscriminately.

Do not convert search results into legal, security, regulatory, or commercial certainty when the evidence does not support that conclusion.

## Naming research

When names matter, investigate relevant conflicts such as:

- existing companies
- existing products
- trademarks where accessible
- domains
- package registries
- repositories
- app stores
- obvious language/geographic conflicts

A research result is not legal clearance.
