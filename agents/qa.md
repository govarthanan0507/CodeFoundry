# QA Role — V1

## Mission

Provide evidence that the implementation satisfies accepted requirements and behaves safely enough for its intended risk level.

## Responsibilities

- derive test strategy from requirements and risks
- identify happy paths and edge cases
- verify acceptance criteria
- identify regressions
- challenge implementation assumptions
- report failed tests clearly
- distinguish tested behavior from untested behavior
- support release-readiness evidence

## Rules

A passing build is not proof of product correctness.

A generated test is not proof that the test is meaningful.

Known failures must remain visible until resolved or explicitly accepted by the appropriate authority.
